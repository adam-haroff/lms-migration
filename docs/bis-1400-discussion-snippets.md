```html
<h2 style="color: #ac1a2f; border-bottom: 10px solid #AC1A2F; padding: 10px;"><img src="https://sinclair.instructure.com/courses/17038/files/516644/download" alt="" width="45" height="45" data-decorative="true" data-api-endpoint="https://sinclair.instructure.com/api/v1/courses/17038/files/516644" data-api-returntype="File"><strong>Discussion Overview</strong></h2>
<p>This graded discussion is worth 10 points. Mystery Shop Part 1 requires you <strong>to post your plans for your Mystery Shop</strong>. Review <a title="What is a Mystery Shop?" href="https://sinclair.instructure.com/courses/17038/pages/what-is-a-mystery-shop" data-course-type="wikiPages" data-published="true" data-api-endpoint="https://sinclair.instructure.com/api/v1/courses/17038/pages/what-is-a-mystery-shop" data-api-returntype="Page">What is a Mystery Shop?</a> first. You can complete your mystery shop in person or online. The Mystery Shop video features a face-to-face mystery shop, but there are lots of options for a remote mystery shop: you could contact a company customer service department to see about expedited delivery date, color not listed, recommendations, etc. <strong>What&rsquo;s due for Part 1 is YOUR PLAN posted in the discussion forum &ndash; not the actual mystery shop.</strong></p>
<p>There is more information about the mystery shop on the <a title="What is a Mystery Shop?" href="https://sinclair.instructure.com/courses/17038/pages/what-is-a-mystery-shop" data-course-type="wikiPages" data-published="true" data-api-endpoint="https://sinclair.instructure.com/api/v1/courses/17038/pages/what-is-a-mystery-shop" data-api-returntype="Page">What is a Mystery Shop?</a> page.</p>
<p>Where students often fall short is on the planned scenario: your plan should go beyond an ordinary shopping experience &ndash; you need to complicate things a little bit to see if you are provided service beyond the minimum. I would assume the Customer Service Representative (CSR) will process your order &ndash; but how does the CSR deal with questions about color options, size options, delivery options, etc.? Does he/she/they stay helpful or do you feel rushed or like you are bothering the rep? To earn full points for this assignment you need to have a plan beyond just a &ldquo;standard&rdquo; purchase.</p>
<p>If you do not want to make an on-site visit for your mystery shop, here are some ideas of how to complete the assignment remotely. You can call or use the Chat feature if the company offers one. Maybe contact the customer service department at any company with whom you do business or would consider doing business.</p>
<ul>
<li>Ask questions about your bill (or a parent's bill if they approve and you don't have accounts of your own), or about new promotions to decrease you bill.</li>
<li>If something was ordered online, contact the company about the order - was it easy to get information using a tracking number or did you have to enter/give your information multiple times?</li>
<li>Another example, call Spectrum or Cincinnati Bell and find out what packages are available in your area, see if you can get a better promotion than the rep first tells you about. If the rep offers you a bundle with phone, Internet, and TV - can you get the same prices per item with only 2 of them? What if you have a contract with another carrier - will they help you pay off your contract?</li>
<li>If you are not purchasing, as you close the conversation (chat or phone) see if you can get contact information to contact the same rep to see how the rep handles that.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Prompt</h3>
<p>For your Mystery Shop Before post, first, choose a business, then research the company to find information about who they are and what they do. Provide complete information which reflect the questions they answer. Here are the minimal questions you can answer about the company:</p>
<ol>
<li>Why did you choose this particular business to conduct a mystery shop?</li>
<li>When was the company founded?</li>
<li>Where is the corporate office located and how many locations do they have?</li>
<li>How many people are employed by the business?</li>
<li>If they have a website, list the web address. Can a customer make purchases on the site? Is the site easy to use?</li>
<li>Do they have a mission statement or company vision? If so, what is it?</li>
<li>Is the business publicly traded on the stock exchange?</li>
<li>Are there any current complaints posted about the company? Use one of the complaints websites to see if there are any current issues.</li>
<li>Do they have a social presence? Facebook, Twitter, etc.</li>
<li>What is your planned scenario? How will you "complicate" a typical purchase or request for service to see if the rep will go beyond the basics?</li>
</ol>
```
